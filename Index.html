Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 30, 2025

Status Saver Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our app handles your information.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Status Saver Studio does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.

The app requests access to your device storage only to save WhatsApp statuses that you choose to download. This data is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

We may use internet access to display ads, but we do not transmit any user data to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may display ads from third-party networks. These networks may use cookies or identifiers to serve personalized ads based on your interests. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
